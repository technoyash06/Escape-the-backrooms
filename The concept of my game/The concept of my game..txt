The concept

You play as a mysterious creature that has three forms, and each form has a completely different ability.

But here's the twist:

You cannot use all abilities at once.

You have to constantly change your form at the right moment to overcome obstacles.

The three forms

 Swift Form

Very fast
Can double jump
Can pass through small gaps
Weak

 Titan Form

Very slow
Extremely strong
Can break walls
Can activate heavy pressure plates
Cannot jump high

 Sky Form

Can fly/glide
Can reach high areas
Can activate things from the air
Very fragile
 The main mechanic

Imagine a level like this:

             🔑
              |
          ─────────
             ↑
          🦅 Sky
             |
     ┌───────┴───────┐
     │               │
    🧱              🕳️
  WALL             HOLE
     │               │
     └───────┬───────┘
             |
          ⚖️ SWITCH
             |
           🚪 EXIT

You need to:

Titan → smash the wall 
↓
Swift → run through the hole 
↓
Sky → fly up and grab the key 
↓
Titan → activate the heavy switch 
↓
 Escape

So the player isn't just asking:

"Where do I go?"

They're asking:

"Which form should I be right now?"

That's your game's identity.

 Add one mechanic that makes it competition-worthy
Momentum Shifting

This is the feature I'd use to make your game stand out.

When you transform, you keep your momentum.

For example:

 Swift form
→ run toward a gap
→ transform into 🦅 Sky form while moving
→ launch yourself across the gap.

Or:

 Swift
→ run toward a cracked wall
→  Titan
→ smash through it with your momentum.

Now transformation becomes part of movement, not just a menu.

That gives you some really satisfying gameplay moments.

 Add an enemy: The Null

Instead of having lots of enemies, have one interesting enemy.

The Null

A creature that copies your current form.

If you're:

 Swift → Null becomes fast.

 Titan → Null becomes strong.

 Sky → Null can fly.

So you have to think strategically.

For example:

"If I transform into Titan, the enemy becomes Titan too... so maybe I should transform into Sky and escape instead."

This creates another layer of strategy without requiring complicated enemy AI.

 Story

Keep the story simple enough that you can explain it easily.

The world

Your character is the last Shifter, a creature capable of changing its physical form.

An ancient machine called the Null Engine is draining the world of its energy.

You must reach the engine and destroy it.

But the engine has divided the world into three environments:

 Zone 1 — The Overgrowth

Introduces the forms.

 Zone 2 — The Ruins

More complicated puzzles.

 Zone 3 — The Machine

Combines everything and introduces the Null.

🔴 Final Zone — The Core

A short boss/puzzle sequence where you have to switch forms rapidly.

You don't need a giant story.

The gameplay is the story.

🧠 Why this is a good competition project

Judges can understand the entire game from one sentence:

"It's a puzzle-platformer where you switch between three forms, each with different abilities, to overcome obstacles."

Then you demonstrate it.

That's MUCH better than spending five minutes explaining complicated lore.

And you can explain your design choices:

Problem

"Different obstacles require different abilities."

Solution

"The player can instantly transform between three forms."

Challenge

"The player has to choose the correct form and timing."

Unique mechanic

"Momentum is preserved during transformations, allowing advanced movement combinations."

That's a very clean presentation.
